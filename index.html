The Punishment Wheel

Finishing last isn't just embarrassing — it's a whole ordeal.

  • Last place in the regular season standings has to face the Punishment Wheel in the offseason.
  • Each year the league votes on 9–10 punishment ideas. Any owner can propose swapping one out — needs 7/12 to pass.
  • The last-place team picks 3 punishments from the approved list to load onto the wheel (~33% each), then gets one spin.
  • Whatever it lands on has to be completed with photo/video/receipt proof in the group chat before the next draft.
  • No proof by draft day = you forfeit your spot in the league for next season.
  • The Toilet Bowl loser also owes an extra $25 on top of everything else.
